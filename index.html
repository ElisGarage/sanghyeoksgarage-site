The Hunt

Target No. 1 · The 1-of-1 CREGiT

Korea built a supercar and the world barely noticed. The Spirra was hand-built in South Korea — a mid-engine, rear-drive machine from a tiny company that refused to accept Korea couldn't make a supercar. Only 31 Spirras were ever made.

And in Daegu, one car sits above them all: a yellow CREGiT with black stripes, 1,538 km on the clock, the only one of its kind — 1 of 1.

This store exists for one reason: to bring that car home to Houston. Every shirt, every poster, every diecast is a brick in that road.

The Car

Why a Spirra?

The Origin — Proto Motors PS-II, V8 Concept

Before the Spirra, there was the PS-II — a mid-engine prototype built by Han-chul Kim's Proto Motors, powered by a Ford Mustang SVT Cobra V8 making about 320 hp. In 2007, Oullim Motors acquired Proto — and the PS-II's DNA became the Spirra.

Long before Korea was known for performance cars, a small Korean company called Oullim decided to build a supercar by hand. The result was the Spirra — a mid-engine machine built in tiny numbers and sold almost entirely at home, which is why most of the world never heard of it.

Only 31 Spirras were ever made. The hotter variants — the T, the EX — are rarer still. And at the very top sits the CREGiT: a one-off, 470hp, yellow with black stripes, and the only one ever made. It's sitting in Daegu, South Korea.

The Hunt is Eli's mission to buy that car and bring it to Houston, Texas — documented video by video on the Sanghyeok’s 차고 (Eli's Garage) channel. This store exists for one reason: every tee, poster, and sticker moves the CREGiT fund forward.

New in the garage · 1 of 1 prototype

De Macross Epique GT1

The ghost. Korea tried to build a supercar twice — and almost nobody knows about the second one. In 2009, Korean businessman Jahong Hur founded De Macross and hired Canada's Multimatic to engineer his dream: a hand-formed aluminum body, gullwing doors, and a supercharged Ford V8 built by Roush Yates, claimed at 845 hp by the manufacturer.

It debuted at the Dubai Motor Show in 2011 to real headlines. Then it vanished. Production never happened. Exactly one Epique GT1 exists — a 1-of-1 prototype, the ghost of Korean supercars.

Engine5.4L supercharged Ford V8 · Roush Yates
Power845 hp — manufacturer claim
