Watch the hunt ↗

The Car: Why a Spirra?

The Spirra started life as the PS-II, a concept from Proto Motors, a small Korean sports-car maker. Oullim Motors took it into production as the Spirra and built it by hand, in tiny numbers.

Only 31 were ever made, from the naturally aspirated N to the twin-turbo EX, plus one CREGiT.

  • Spirra NNaturally aspirated~175 hp
  • Spirra SSmall single turbo~330 hp
  • Spirra TBigger turbo~420 hp
  • Spirra EXTwin turbo~500 hp
  • CREGiT1 of 1470 hp

31 made in total.
